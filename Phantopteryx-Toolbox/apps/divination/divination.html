What this is

Zi Wei Dou Shu (紫微斗数) here is a learning-oriented chart shell: solar → lunar, Life / Body palaces, twelve houses on branches — not a commercial fortune engine.

What you can do

  • Enter Gregorian date and hour, then Run chart (or the dock Chart tile).
  • Read the summary lines and the 4×3 palace grid on the white panel.
  • Scroll for Chart AI assistant below the grid: auto summary, chat, and browser local demo model.
  • Switch to I Ching (易经) for coin hexagrams and hexagram chat.

Steps

  1. Keep Zi Wei (紫微) selected in Module.
  2. Adjust Year / Month / Day / Hour, then Run chart.
  3. Scroll down for the Chart AI assistant; use Back to Start Page to exit.

I Ching: cast coins and reset in the center Hex panel. Solar note uses the fields below.（易经掷钱与重置在 Hex；简批用下列公历。）

What this is

This panel is the I Ching (Book of Changes) corner of the divination toolbox: you cast six lines (like three-coin throws) to get a hexagram, optional changing lines, and a short reading.

What you can do

  • Build a hexagram line by line or in one tap (quick cast).
  • See the main hexagram, moving lines, and the changed hexagram when applicable.
  • Read the built-in analysis and chat with preset prompts or your own question (offline templates, or an optional compatible API).
  • Use the date fields above for a light Solar / calendar side note (zodiac sign, Western constellation, traditional double-hour branch)—for fun, not a full chart.

Steps

  1. Select I Ching (易经) in the middle Module row so Hex appears.
  2. In Hex, press Quick cast for six lines at once, or Cast next line six times (bottom → top).
  3. Read the cards on the right; scroll to Hexagram notes (卦象解析), presets, and custom chat.
  4. Reset clears everything. Back to Start Page returns to the main toolbox.
