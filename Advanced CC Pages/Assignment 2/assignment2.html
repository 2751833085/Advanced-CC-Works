Assignment 2: Words in Motion

A compact archive of three motion studies originally built in P5JS.

I. Minimize

Three sketches explored reduction, disappearance, and controlled visual compression. The final direction focused on making the screen feel increasingly quiet as interaction continued.

II. Procrastinate

Three sketches treated delay as motion: loops, hesitation, repeated starts, and visual systems that avoid completion while still appearing active.

III. Irony

Three sketches used contradiction between text, movement, and timing. The word was presented through mismatched behavior rather than a literal illustration.

Archive Note
